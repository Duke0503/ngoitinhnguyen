## 4. Domain model in business terms

What the website has to represent, derived from §2 and §3:

```
Organisation
 └─ Term ("Mùa", nhiệm kỳ)  ── BTC, departments, members
     └─ Campaign (chương trình)  e.g. TTYT26 "Đèn Trăng 2"
         ├─ series / edition         Đèn Trăng → Đèn Trăng 2 (yearly series: Trung Thu, Xuân, 20/10…)
         ├─ code                     TTYT26, used in tags [TTYT26] and transfer memos
         ├─ slogan, story, cover
         ├─ phases                   Gói đèn → Ngòi dệt → Trông trăng (named, dated, may be revised)
         ├─ fund(s)                  "Dệt sắc xuân xanh", "Mặt trời nhỏ" (named, with a bank account + memo format)
         ├─ budget (dự trù)          line items
         ├─ branches / field teams   "Chi nhánh Hà Nội", "Đội hình Xuân Gia Lai" (region ≠ province!)
         │    └─ locations (địa bàn)   address, date(s), access notes (e.g. island, by boat)
         │         └─ activities        "Tô thắm điểm trường", "Mùa trăng trao tay"…
         │              └─ results        gifts, lanterns, scholarships (by type), meals, books, money value, people served
         ├─ fundraising channels     cash, goods wish list, products, workshop tickets, services, sponsor-matched pledges
         ├─ sponsors & partners      tier or custom title; partners (Đoàn, CLB, VNV) are separate from paying sponsors
         ├─ recruitment rounds       TNV for this campaign or team
         ├─ people & roles           scoped as in §3.3
         ├─ engagement               letter box, "Trung thu trong bạn là gì?" comments, profile frame
         └─ updates (posts)          10–30 per campaign
```
